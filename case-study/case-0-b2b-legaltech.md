# Case 0: a B2B legaltech

Methodology validation, not a controlled study.

## Context

A B2B legaltech selling to companies ran the method behind Perlead on its own business development: one agent per role (prospecting, post-send follow-up, proposals), a dossier per lead, follow-ups written from each lead's history with no templates, a judge step before every draft, and a human approving and sending every message.

## Reported results (about 10 days)

| Metric | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Email reply rate | 7%+ |
| LinkedIn reply rate | 33%+ |
| Calls | 6 |
| Proposals | 3 |
| Pipeline | USD 15k+ |

## Caveats

- These are numbers reported by the company, not audited.
- No per-channel sample size or formal baseline was published for this period.
- The period is short and includes warm and cold leads.
- It validates that the method can work in a real B2B sales motion. It is not a promise of results for anyone else.
