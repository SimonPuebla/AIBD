# Weekly review (stub)

Status: stub in v0.1.

- Read the ledger (`10-case-study-logger`) and dossiers for the week.
- Report: drafts created, drafts sent by the owner, human replies by class and by touch number, meetings, proposals, leads stopped and why, deals with no next step.
- Count the owner's edits to drafts and the judge's revise and block reasons. An edit or reason that repeats 3 times becomes a proposed rule.
- Propose at most one rule change and one experiment per week. The owner approves before anything changes.
- Never count out-of-office, bounces or unsent drafts as results. Always show sample size.
