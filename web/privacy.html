Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026

The short version

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. Perlead itself runs inside your own AI agent, inbox and CRM: we never receive your leads, emails or CRM data.

What we receive

  • Purchases. Creem, our merchant of record, processes your payment and shares with us your name, email, country and order details so we can deliver and support your purchase. We never see your full card number. Creem's own privacy policy covers its processing.
  • Email. If you write to us, we keep the conversation to answer you.
  • Setup sessions. We see what you share on the call. We do not record calls unless you agree first.
  • Hosting. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security, for a limited time.

Why and how long

We use this data only to deliver what you bought, support you and meet tax and accounting duties. We keep order records as long as the law requires and delete correspondence when it is no longer needed. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not send marketing email without your consent.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use, by emailing simonpueblafin@gmail.com. Residents of Argentina can also contact the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (Law 25.326). Residents of the EU and UK have the rights set out in the GDPR, including complaining to their data protection authority.
