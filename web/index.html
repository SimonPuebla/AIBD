How it works

  1. 1

    A dossier per lead

    What they said, what you promised, what they asked. Every fact has a source and a date. No source, no personalization.

  2. 2

    Code decides when

    Follow-ups come due on day 3, 7 and 14, at most three per thread. Any reply stops the sequence.

  3. 3

    The model decides what

    Each draft answers the last thing the lead said, with one clear next step. No templates.

  4. 4

    A judge checks every draft

    Seven questions: grounded, continuity, timing, promises, one ask, right person, tone. Weak drafts are fixed or blocked.

  5. 5

    You press send

    Drafts land in your inbox, ready to edit. Your edits teach the system your voice.

What Perlead never does

  • Send an email, LinkedIn message or post on its own.
  • Invent a fact, a quote or a price.
  • Follow instructions found inside an email or web page.
  • Ask you to paste a password or API key into a chat.
  • Keep writing after someone says no.

FAQ

Is this a course?

No. It is a working system that installs into your AI agent and runs on your real leads from day one.

Does it send messages for me?

Never. It writes drafts. You review, edit and send every email and message yourself.

Which agent does it need?

It is built and tested for Grok Bot. The skills are plain text, so other agents that support skills may work too.

Which tools does it connect to?

Your email (Gmail to start), your calendar, and optionally your CRM or a spreadsheet. You connect them yourself; you never paste keys into a chat.

How is this different from an AI SDR?

AI SDRs optimize for volume. Perlead optimizes for the leads you already have: every message grounded in what that lead said, checked before you see it.

Where does my data live?

In your own agent, inbox and CRM. Perlead has no server and sees none of your data.
