Method

Deals are rarely lost to a weak pitch. They are lost to silence.

Follow-ups slip after a good call. Templates tell buyers you forgot what they said. Volume tools send more and listen less. Perlead does the opposite: fewer messages, each one specific.

  1. 01

    A record for every lead

    What they said, what you promised, what they asked, each with its source and date.

  2. 02

    Rules decide when

    Follow-ups come due on fixed days, at most three per thread, and stop on any reply.

  3. 03

    The record decides what

    Each draft answers the last thing the lead said and asks for one next step.

  4. 04

    Every draft is reviewed

    Seven checks before you see it: grounded, continuity, timing, promises, one ask, right person, tone.

  5. 05

    You send

    Drafts wait in your inbox. Your edits refine the voice over time.

FAQ

Questions

Is this a course?

No. It is a working system that installs into your AI agent and runs on your real pipeline from the first day.

Does it send messages for me?

No. It prepares drafts. You review, edit and send every email and message yourself.

What happens after I buy?

You receive a download link by email. Attach the file to a chat with your agent and paste the install message included in it. Your agent shows you every skill and waits for your OK before installing.

Which agent does it need?

It is built and tested for Grok Bot. The skills are plain text, so other agents that support skills may work as well.

Which tools does it connect to?

Your email (Gmail first), your calendar, and optionally your CRM or a spreadsheet. You connect them through your agent; you never paste keys into a chat.

How is it different from an AI SDR?

AI SDRs are built for volume. Perlead is built for the leads you already have: each message grounded in what that lead said and reviewed before you see it.

Where does my data live?

In your own agent, inbox and CRM. Perlead runs no server and does not see your data. Details in our privacy notice.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Full refund on the Pack within 14 days, and on Setup if you cancel at least 24 hours before the session. See the refund policy.
